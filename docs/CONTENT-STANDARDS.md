# ADAH content standards

Every published tool, service, article, news item or resource must have:
- Stable unique slug and a clear Arabic and English title where applicable.
- Accurate description, category, canonical route and relevant icon/image.
- Named source and publication/update date for sourced claims and news.
- Correct links and no duplicate or thin content.
- Reviewed translation, including RTL/LTR behavior.
- A visible last-updated date when freshness matters.
- A moderation and publication status.

News without a trustworthy source and timestamp must not be published. Usage rankings must come from measured, privacy-minimized events, never fabricated counters. User ratings and reports are moderated before public display.
